# Code of Conduct

This project is a friendly place to learn and build. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience level, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask questions when something is unclear.
- Accept decisions gracefully, even when you disagree.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing unrelated self-promotion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, email **thevigesh17@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential.
